Specs

106 × 19.5 × 7 mm
Closure
Sliding lid; pull the 5 mm tab at the open end to extract

Print notes

  • No supports needed. Body prints with the cavity up; the lid groove uses a 45° chamfered ceiling so every layer is supported.
  • Print both flat on the bed at 0.2 mm layers, 2–3 perimeters, 15–20% infill.
  • PLA or PETG. Avoid flexible filaments — the lid needs to stay rigid.
  • If your printer over-extrudes, the lid may bind on first insertion. Slide it in and out a few times to wear in the fit, or scale the lid 99% on X/Y.
