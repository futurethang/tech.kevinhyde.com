Print notes

  • Print both parts flat (largest face down). No supports needed.
  • 0.2 mm layer height, 2–3 perimeters, 20% infill is plenty.
  • Cap clearance is 0.2 mm per side — if your printer runs tight, scale the cap by ~101% on X/Y.
  • Any rigid filament works (PLA, PETG). Avoid flexibles.
